03 / Profile

About Franco

Design and systems that make good work possible.

I am a 31-year-old architect who grew up in Stellenbosch, a place whose landscape, heritage, and strong sense of community deeply shaped my appreciation for the built environment.

I have always had a deep passion for the art of creating and design. I pursue architecture not simply as a profession, but as a calling—a discipline that allows me to express ideas in ways that add tangible value to society, from small-scale interventions to complex commercial developments. My goal is to make a visible and meaningful impact on the built environment and the communities it serves, building a career that is both worthwhile and rewarding.

My interest in architecture stems from a strong curiosity about the intersection of art and engineering—the ability to translate abstract ideas into functional, enduring spaces. I am particularly energised by the challenges and constraints within projects; I see them not as limitations, but as catalysts for innovative and thoughtful solutions.

In addition to my passion for design, I have developed a strong interest in the business side of architecture. I am deeply engaged in client relationships, fee structuring, financial performance, and company cashflow management. I actively pursue the systemisation and optimisation of internal processes to improve efficiency—with the clear intention of creating more space and freedom for meaningful design work. I believe that strong business foundations enable stronger architecture.

I would describe myself as confident, optimistic, enthusiastic, and creative. I thrive in environments that challenge me and push me to grow. I am adaptable, driven, and committed to continuous learning. I believe my combination of design sensitivity, technical competence, business awareness, and dedication to the profession would allow me to contribute meaningful value to your team while continuing to develop as an architectural professional.
